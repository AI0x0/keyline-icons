0.11.2

Released · 20 Sept 2026

Two redraws for the bare symbols. plus and minus come in to 18 from 20, the box puzzle, pause and stop already stand at: a cross and a bar lay the least ink of anything drawn on the full 22 box -- the plus paints 80 where its neighbours there paint two and three hundred -- so the eye takes the measure of the ground they claim rather than the marks on it, and in a row of icons they read a size larger than they are. The plus inside the circle and square containers is a different drawing and is untouched.

0.7.1

Released · 8 Sept 2026

Redrawn for the editing toolbar. The clip family stands taller: split and both trims grow their frames from 8 to 12 units so the halves read as portrait clips, and the trims' cross-outs follow. 0.7.0

Released · 8 Sept 2026

Sized for menus. The bare symbols — plus, minus, search, the arrows, the fullscreen pair — now fill their 22 box, every horizontal stands 18 tall, the dashed frames and rings run their dashes at the two-unit gap, and film and bounding-box lose weight. With it, the editing set: clip trims and splits, keyframes, camera start and end, the gesture marks.
