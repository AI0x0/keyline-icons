0.7.1

Released · 8 Sept 2026

Redrawn for the editing toolbar. The clip family stands taller: split and both trims grow their frames from 8 to 12 units so the halves read as portrait clips, and the trims' cross-outs follow. Mouse-move now lights the scroll wheel instead of the left button, saying middle-drag the same way the wheel icon says wheel. Scale-frame closes its frame and nests the corner inside, shipping duotone and fill for the first time.

4 drawings added since 0.7.0 without a new name, taking the set from 3,678 drawings to 3,682. 21 redrawn. Every drawing of this release is on keylineicons.com/changelog.

0.7.0

Released · 8 Sept 2026

Sized for menus. The bare symbols — plus, minus, search, the arrows, the fullscreen pair — now fill their 22 box, every horizontal stands 18 tall, the dashed frames and rings run their dashes at the two-unit gap, and film and bounding-box lose weight. Drawn against the rows of a real editor, where 14-tall and 22-tall glyphs used to sit side by side. With it, the editing set: clip trims and splits, keyframes, camera start and end, the gesture marks.

39 drawings added since 0.6.0, bringing the set to 721, and 119 redrawn. Every drawing of this release is on keylineicons.com/changelog.
